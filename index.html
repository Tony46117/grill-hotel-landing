Our Story

A love letter to live fire

Mean Grill began with a single charcoal pit and a stubborn belief: great meat needs nothing but flame, salt and time.

Today our chefs master the ancient art of fire cooking — searing prime cuts over glowing embers, smoking brisket for sixteen hours and grilling vegetables until they sing. Leave full, rested and happy.

  • Certified Angus & grass-fed Kenyan beef
  • Hand-cut butchery on site, daily
  • Dine-in, takeaway & event hosting
  • Free valet parking & private dining
