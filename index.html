Our Story

A love letter to live fire

Ember & Oak began with a single charcoal pit and a stubborn belief: great meat needs nothing but flame, salt and time.

Today our chefs master the ancient art of fire cooking — searing prime cuts over glowing embers, smoking brisket for sixteen hours and grilling vegetables until they sing. Stay overnight in our lodge rooms, wake to the scent of morning coals, and leave full, rested and happy.

  • Certified Angus & grass-fed Kenyan beef
  • Hand-cut butchery on site, daily
  • 12 luxury lodge rooms with garden views
  • Free valet parking & event hosting
